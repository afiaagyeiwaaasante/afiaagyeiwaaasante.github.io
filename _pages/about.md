I am a PhD student in Information Systems – Cyber Security at Dakota State University and a Graduate Research Assistant with the Center for Quantum Information Science & Technology (C-QIST), Madison Cyber Labs.

My research focuses on the protection of critical information infrastructure, with emphasis on quantum-secure communication and machine learning for attack detection. I study how quantum key distribution (QKD) protocols can be applied to critical infrastructure, model adversaries against these systems, and apply quantum and classical machine learning to detect attacks, building on prior work in secure software engineering and explainable malware detection.

Before my PhD, I served as Principal Manager (Director) for Critical Information Infrastructure at Ghana's Cyber Security Authority, where I led the development of a sector-wide risk management framework for critical systems.
